Data Deletion Instructions

SpeakZen – English Speaking App

SpeakZen does not require user login or account creation. We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

Data We Collect

We may collect non-personal and anonymous data for improving app performance, including:

  • App usage data (analytics)
  • Crash reports and device information
  • Push notification tokens

For in-app purchases, all transactions are securely handled by Google Play. We do not store any payment details.

Data Deletion

Since we do not store personal user data or maintain user accounts, there is no user-specific data to delete.

You can stop all data collection by uninstalling the app from your device.

Third-Party Control

You can manage or delete your data through:

  • Your Google account settings
  • Resetting your device advertising ID
